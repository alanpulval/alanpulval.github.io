Alan Pulval-Dady

Research Institute CODE · Universität der Bundeswehr München

I work on privacy-enhancing cryptographic protocols and post-quantum cryptography, particularly anonymous credentials, blind signatures, and MPC-in-the-Head proof techniques. I am a PhD student at the Research Institute CODE, Universität der Bundeswehr München, supervised by Mark Manulis and Daniel Slamanig.
